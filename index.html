Good to know

PLAYVERSE questions

What is PLAYVERSE?

PLAYVERSE is a growing browser-gaming destination featuring original games and experiences made to play directly on the web.

Is it free to play?

Yes. The games currently listed on PLAYVERSE are free to access. Any future optional paid features will be clearly labeled.

What can I play right now?

Explore VELOCITY for 3D driving, OPEN WORLD for city exploration, and PIXEL FOUNDRY for an arcade-style challenge.

Does PLAYVERSE have paid memberships?

Not yet. Premium memberships, purchases, and advertising are future possibilities, not active services. Never pay anyone claiming to sell PLAYVERSE upgrades.
